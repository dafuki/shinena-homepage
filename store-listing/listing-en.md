## Full description

```
Shinena turns Torah study into a game you can actually keep up with.

Recite a mishna, a verse or a daf out loud, and Shinena checks you word by
word, awards XP, and puts the passage back on your screen right before you
would have forgotten it.

LEARN THE WHOLE CORPUS — OFFLINE
• All 63 tractates of the Mishna
• The complete Tanakh — Torah, Nevi'im and Ketuvim
• The Babylonian Talmud
• Rambam (Mishneh Torah), all 11 volumes
• Shulchan Arukh — all fourTurim
Every text downloads once on first launch (~11 MB), then works fully
offline — learn on the bus, on a flight, or with no signal. No page
reloads, no waiting, no data bill.

AN ADVENTURE WORLD MAP
Every chapter is a stage on a winding trail. Pick a tractate, walk the map,
unlock the next stage — Mishna, Tanakh and the Talmud each get their own
world, with your lion mascot travelling between the milestones.

RECITE OUT LOUD, GET SCORED IN REAL TIME
Shinena's Hebrew speech recognition understands niqqud. It checks your
recitation against the text as you speak, so you find the mispronounced word
while it is still fresh — and your voice never leaves the device unless you
ask us to score it.

SMART REVIEW (SM-2)
The scheduling algorithm decides when each passage should come back, based on
how well you knew it. Practise for ten minutes a day and your memory keeps
the whole passage — for years.

STREAKS, BADGES AND LEADERBOARDS
Keep a daily streak, collect badges, and compare yourself with players
worldwide. The leaderboard breaks down by country, by subject and by book,
so you always compete against someone at your own level.

14 THEMES
From a forest quest to a retro arcade, there are 14 themes to make the
arena yours. Switch any time — your progress is never lost.

BUILT FOR SERIOUS LEARNERS
• Graduated hints — from a first letter to a full recitation
• Combos, stars and daily goals
• Hebrew and English interface
• Works on phones and tablets, portrait or landscape

Shinena is free, has no ads and no in-app purchases. Donations keep the
servers and the sources running.

Download Shinena and start your first chapter today.
```
